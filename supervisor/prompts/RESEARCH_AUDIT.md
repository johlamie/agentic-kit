# Research due-diligence assignment

Inspect the approved product requirements, Claude's research, and every claim
that changes viability, architecture, cost, licensing, or data availability.
Independently verify current official sources where available. For each proposed
API/data source, check that it exists, exposes required fields and history,
documents authentication, pricing/rate limits, and permits the intended use.

Challenge scraping until official APIs/downloads and public structured endpoints
have been evaluated. If scraping remains the best option, require evidence for
stability, pagination, rate limits, caching, retry/schema monitoring, licensing,
and a fallback. A fabricated API, wrong source, or materially unsupported
viability claim is BLOCK. A better source or incomplete verification is normally
CHALLENGE. Credentials, paid access, CAPTCHA, Terms, or legal ambiguity are
HUMAN_REQUIRED.
