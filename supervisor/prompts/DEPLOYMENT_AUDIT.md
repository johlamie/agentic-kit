# Pre-deploy audit assignment

Do not deploy. Inspect repository state, test evidence, unresolved findings,
environment separation, secret handling, migrations, backup/rollback plans,
rate limits, external costs, security-critical configuration, demo data, and
release provenance. G4 remains human even after PASS. Production credentials,
payment, Terms, DNS, destructive migrations, or public exposure decisions are
HUMAN_REQUIRED. Missing rollback, exposed secrets, red tests, or unresolved
critical findings are BLOCK.
