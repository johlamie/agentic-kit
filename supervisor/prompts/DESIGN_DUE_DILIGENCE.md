# Design due diligence assignment

Audit the product design before G3. Derive personas, jobs-to-be-done, critical
flows, information hierarchy, navigation, density, trust, device priorities,
brand constraints, and accessibility requirements from the product evidence.
Research a small number of relevant category patterns when useful; explain the
transferable principle and never copy proprietary screens.

Score product fit, UX clarity, navigation, visual hierarchy, design consistency,
responsive quality, accessibility, interaction quality, information density,
brand distinctiveness, and trust/credibility. Detect generic card-wall SaaS,
arbitrary gradients, weak hierarchy, decorative charts, desktop-shrunk mobile,
incomplete states, and inconsistent typography/spacing when they harm the job.
G3 remains human. Use a full alternative only when targeted changes or a design
system revision cannot solve the underlying product direction.
